First Aid Guide

Learn simple first-aid information for common emergency situations. Stay calm, protect yourself, and get professional emergency help when needed.

⚠️ Important Safety Notice

First aid is basic emergency support and does not replace professional medical care. For serious or life-threatening emergencies, contact emergency services immediately.

🩸

Bleeding

For minor bleeding, basic first aid can help protect the wound while professional help is arranged if needed.

Basic steps

  1. Make sure the area is safe before helping.
  2. Use clean cloth or gauze to apply gentle, steady pressure to the wound.
  3. Keep the wound covered with a clean dressing.
  4. Get medical help if bleeding is heavy, persistent, or the injury is serious.
⚠️ Avoid touching a wound with unclean hands.
🔥

Burns

Burns can happen from heat, hot liquids, electricity, chemicals, or other sources.

Basic steps

  1. Move away from the source of heat if it is safe.
  2. Cool a minor burn with cool running water.
  3. Keep the affected area protected with a clean, non-stick covering.
  4. Seek medical attention for serious or extensive burns.
⚠️ Do not apply household substances such as toothpaste or butter to a burn.
🦴

Fracture / Injury

A suspected fracture or serious injury should be handled carefully to avoid making the injury worse.

Basic steps

  1. Keep the injured person as still and comfortable as possible.
  2. Avoid unnecessary movement of the injured area.
  3. Do not try to straighten a visibly injured limb.
  4. Seek professional medical assistance.
⚠️ Do not attempt to push a bone or joint back into place.
😵

Fainting

If someone faints, make sure the surrounding area is safe and check whether they respond normally.

Basic steps

  1. Make sure the person is in a safe position.
  2. Check whether they respond and are breathing normally.
  3. Give them space and keep the surroundings calm.
  4. Seek emergency help if they do not recover normally or have other concerning symptoms.
⚠️ Do not give food or drink to someone who is unconscious.
😮

Choking

Choking can block a person's airway and may become life-threatening very quickly.

Basic steps

  1. Encourage the person to cough if they can breathe and cough effectively.
  2. If they cannot breathe or speak, get emergency help immediately.
  3. Follow the emergency operator's instructions while waiting for help.
  4. Stay with the person and monitor their condition.
⚠️ Severe choking requires immediate emergency assistance.
⚡

Electric Shock

Electrical accidents can be dangerous even when there are no obvious injuries.

Basic steps

  1. Do not touch the person while they may still be connected to the electrical source.
  2. Make sure the power source has been safely switched off before approaching.
  3. Contact emergency services for significant electrical injuries.
  4. Keep the person safe and monitor them while waiting for professional help.
⚠️ Never put yourself at risk by approaching an active electrical source.

🚨 Need Immediate Emergency Help?

If the situation is serious or life-threatening, contact emergency services immediately instead of relying only on first-aid information.
